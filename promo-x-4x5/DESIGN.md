# Motion Sticker Pack — X 4:5 Promo

## Style Prompt

Playful product-demo motion graphics for a creator tool: warm paper-like light canvas, rounded sticker cards, chunky friendly typography, simple geometric mascot, bright orange and sky blue accents, small purple and yellow highlights, and quick but readable springy motion. The composition should feel approachable to a first-time creator while still showing a precise, reliable workflow.

## Colors

- `#F8F5EE` — warm paper background
- `#18252F` — primary text and outlines
- `#D67433` — action / output accent
- `#54B8FF` — workflow / input accent
- `#A77BFF` — secondary highlight
- `#FFD35C` — playful emphasis

## Typography

- `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Avenir Next", "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", sans-serif`
- Heavy rounded display text for the hook; medium-weight body text for process labels.

## Motion Rules

- Use short spring entrances and gentle scale pops.
- Keep the central product story readable at every moment.
- Use the progress line and color changes to show sequence, not decoration.

## What NOT to Do

- No dark enterprise dashboard aesthetic.
- No tiny technical labels that cannot be read in an X feed.
- No dense code or terminal screenshots in the first version.
- No aggressive camera shake or full-screen flashing transitions.
