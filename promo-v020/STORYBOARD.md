# V0.2.0 Sticker Promo — beat plan

## 0–3s · Hook / five worlds arrive

The frame is a warm paper launchpad. Five characters punch into view as a compact constellation while the headline locks in: “V0.2.0 / 表情包，动得更像样”. Coral and aqua registration marks make the upgrade feel tangible, not abstract.

## 3–9s · Real result wall

The five packs fan out into a diagonal product wall. Each character gets a short identity tag and three visible reactions. The viewer should feel immediate variety and shareability.

## 9–15s · Upgrade 01 / independent motion

One oversized sticker card takes the lead while neighboring cards step back. A precise label reads “每格独立动作”. Three reaction stills snap in sequence around it to show that one pack is not one repeated loop.

## 15–21s · Upgrade 02 / stable transparency + smoother loops

Checkerboard transparency becomes a clean product surface. A bright outline traces the sticker silhouette while two small status chips appear: “透明更稳” and “循环更顺”. The movement breathes instead of shaking.

## 21–26s · Upgrade 03 / delivery formats

The five characters compress into a tidy export rail. Chips count in: “GIF / WebP”, then “3s / 6s”. The delivery promise lands as a tangible export action.

## 26–30s · CTA / V0.2.0 lands

All five characters rise into a final lineup. The message resolves to “从想法，到可发送” with a small “V0.2.0 · now moving” stamp. Hold the final frame for recognition and sharing.

## Rhythm

fast → fan-out → focused proof → calm confidence → export snap → warm hold.

## Sound

Use the existing light promotional music bed. Text and image hits should be readable without depending on audio.
