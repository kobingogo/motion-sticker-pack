# V0.2.0 Sticker Promo

## Direction

Warm paper meets candy-tech. The frame should feel like a finished social asset: tactile paper grain, sticker cutouts, bold rounded Chinese type, fast but controlled spring motion, and a small amount of precise product metadata.

## Palette

- `#FFF8EC` warm paper
- `#1E2530` ink
- `#FF6B4A` coral action
- `#31B8C5` aqua system accent
- `#7B61FF` violet highlight
- `#FFD35A` sun yellow
- `#FFFFFF` card fill

## Typography

- `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Avenir Next", "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", sans-serif`
- 900-weight rounded display headlines; 700-weight labels; 500-weight supporting copy.

## Motion

- Hook lands with a punchy scale/rotation pop.
- Sticker results float, spring, and snap into a grid.
- Upgrade labels reveal like a product changelog, with short wipes and count-up ticks.
- Final five-character lineup holds long enough to read, then ends on a clean CTA.

## Constraints

- 1080×1920 vertical composition, 30 seconds.
- No aggressive flashing, no tiny technical copy, no dark enterprise dashboard look.
